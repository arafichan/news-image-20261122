Sum up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image.

The front page carries six verified stories of the day: COP31 collapses in
Antalya with no climate deal; Iran strikes Bahrain; Mexican forces capture
cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro"; a Russian drone
hits an apartment block near Kyiv; the International Court of Justice rules
Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017; and US stocks
fall into correction as the AI sell-off spreads.

The image is `entry.png`, 1024 by 1024 pixels, built deterministically
from the six headlines above — the same build always writes the same bytes.
